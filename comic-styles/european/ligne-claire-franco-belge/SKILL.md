# Ligne Claire Franco-Belge

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Uniform line weight on all contours
- Flat color fills only (no gradients or hatching)
- Clean, clear line quality
- Realistic backgrounds with simplified characters
- Hard-edged cast shadows

## When to Use
- Adventure, mystery, or clear narrative stories
- When maximum readability and visual clarity is required

## Integration
- Requires `comic-style-memory-system` for line uniformity enforcement
- Strong match with `comic-core` structural rules

---

*Ligne claire is about disciplined simplicity.*
