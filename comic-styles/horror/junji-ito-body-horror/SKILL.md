# Junji Ito Body Horror

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Two-mode structure: Normative baseline → Horror escalation
- Hatching density and irregularity increases with horror intensity
- Body distortion (spirals, elongation, surface proliferation) while preserving fine detail
- Hyper-realistic faces against impossible anatomy
- Controlled contrast as psychological signal

## When to Use
- Horror, body horror, or psychological dread stories
- When the reference image can support escalating distortion

## Technical Rules (from research)
- Hatching modulation based on emotional intensity scalar
- Conformal spiral warping for distortion
- Fine detail preserved inside distorted regions

## Integration
- Requires strong `comic-style-memory-system` for hatching behavior
- Works with `comic-long-sequence-orchestrator` for escalating horror arcs
