# Horror EC Comics Style

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Gothic, atmospheric horror with heavy ink
- Detailed, slightly exaggerated anatomy
- Strong use of shadow and silhouette
- Moralistic or ironic endings
- Classic 1950s comic book texture and line quality

## When to Use
- Classic horror anthology style stories
- When the reference image supports a vintage, cautionary tone

## Integration
- Requires `comic-style-memory-system` for ink density and texture
- Works with `comic-long-sequence-orchestrator` for anthology-style arcs

---

*EC horror uses dread as its primary tool.*
