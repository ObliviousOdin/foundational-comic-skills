# Sin City Graphic Noir

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Pure black and white with one spot color
- Extreme high contrast
- Hard, graphic shadows
- Minimal but impactful use of color
- Frank Miller-inspired noir aesthetic

## When to Use
- Hard-boiled crime, noir, or violent stories
- When the reference image supports extreme contrast treatment

## Integration
- Requires `comic-style-memory-system` for contrast and spot color rules
- Works with `comic-long-sequence-orchestrator`

---

*Sin City treats color as punctuation.*
