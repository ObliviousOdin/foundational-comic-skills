# Painted Prestige Comic

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Fully painted rendering (gouache/oil feel) — **no ink outlines**; edges resolve through value and color
- Realistic anatomy and fabric weight; figures lit like classical portraiture
- Warm museum lighting, controlled palette, soft atmospheric depth
- Panel compositions staged like history paintings; dignified, monumental framing
- Hand-painted caption boxes and restrained lettering; no screentone, no flat fills

## When to Use

- Mythic, reverent, or retrospective stories; heroes seen as history
- When the reference image's mood cue suggests gravity, nostalgia, or awe
- Pairs naturally with `slow-burn-reveal` and `multi-page-chapter` formats

## Integration

- Heavy use of `comic-style-memory-system` for brushwork and lighting grammar
- Requires `comic-character-consistency-system` — painted realism is unforgiving of face drift
