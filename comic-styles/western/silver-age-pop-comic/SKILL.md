# Silver Age Pop Comic

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Dynamic, exaggerated anatomy
- Kirby crackle and energy effects
- Vibrant, saturated colors
- High-action compositions
- Classic Silver Age comic book dynamism

## When to Use
- High-energy superhero or adventure stories
- When the reference image supports dynamic, explosive action

## Integration
- Requires `comic-style-memory-system` for energy effects and color rules
- Works with `comic-long-sequence-orchestrator`
