# Classic Newspaper Comic

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Bold, clean ink lines with strong silhouettes
- Wholesome, gag-oriented storytelling
- Simple, readable panel layouts
- Clear character designs with minimal detail
- Emphasis on visual punchline in final panel

## When to Use
- Light-hearted, comedic, or family-friendly stories
- When the reference image suggests everyday situations with humorous potential

## Integration
- Uses `comic-core` structural contract heavily
- Simpler consistency requirements than dramatic styles

---

*Newspaper comics prioritize clarity and timing above all.*
