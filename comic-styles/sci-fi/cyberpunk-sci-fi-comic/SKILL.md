# Cyberpunk Sci-Fi Comic

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Neon color accents on dark backgrounds
- Rain, reflections, and chrome surfaces
- High-tech, low-life aesthetic
- Dense urban environments with holographic elements
- Sharp, angular linework with digital texture

## When to Use
- Cyberpunk, dystopian, or near-future stories
- When the reference image suggests technology, alienation, or urban decay

## Integration
- Requires `comic-style-memory-system` for lighting and reflection rules
- Works with `comic-long-sequence-orchestrator` for world-building

---

*Cyberpunk uses light as both beauty and warning.*
