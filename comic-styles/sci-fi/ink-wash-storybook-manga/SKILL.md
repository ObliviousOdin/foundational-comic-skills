# Ink Wash Storybook Manga

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Gray ink washes instead of screentone
- Handmade paper texture
- Quiet, contemplative atmosphere
- Soft, painterly rendering
- Minimal but expressive linework

## When to Use
- Quiet, emotional, or literary manga stories
- When the reference image supports a soft, traditional ink wash feel

## Integration
- Requires `comic-style-memory-system` for wash and texture rules
- Works with `comic-long-sequence-orchestrator`

---

*Ink wash treats tone as atmosphere.*
