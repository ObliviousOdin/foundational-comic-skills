# Underground Zine Comix

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Punk and counterculture energy
- Raw, unpolished aesthetic
- Personal and political themes
- Zine-like page composition

## When to Use
- Personal, political, or counterculture stories
- When the reference image supports raw, expressive energy

- Works with `comic-long-sequence-orchestrator` for serialized zine work
