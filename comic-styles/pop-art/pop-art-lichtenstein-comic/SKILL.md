# Pop Art Lichtenstein Comic

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Ben-Day dot patterns
- Primary color fills
- Bold, graphic outlines
- Dramatic, high-contrast compositions
- Self-aware pop art aesthetic

## When to Use
- Stylized, ironic, or pop-culture stories
- When the reference image supports graphic, high-impact treatment

## Integration
- Requires `comic-style-memory-system` for dot and color rules
- Works with `comic-long-sequence-orchestrator` for serialized pop narratives

---

*Pop art treats the panel as a printed object.*
