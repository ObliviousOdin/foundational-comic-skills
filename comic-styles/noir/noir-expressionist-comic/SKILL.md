# Noir Expressionist Comic

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Strong chiaroscuro (high contrast lighting)
- Rain, wet surfaces, and urban night environments
- Cinematic, low-angle, and Dutch tilt compositions
- Heavy emotional atmosphere and moral ambiguity
- Minimal but impactful use of light sources

## When to Use
- Crime, detective, or morally complex stories
- When the reference image suggests tension, isolation, or urban grit

## Integration
- Requires `comic-style-memory-system` for lighting and shadow rules
- Works well with `comic-long-sequence-orchestrator` for serialized noir

---

*Noir uses light as a character.*
