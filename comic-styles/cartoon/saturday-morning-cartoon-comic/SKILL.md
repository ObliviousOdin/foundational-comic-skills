# Saturday Morning Cartoon Comic

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Bold, uniform cel outlines (consistent weight, closed shapes)
- Flat cel color fills with simple two-tone shading; saturated, optimistic palette
- Squash-and-stretch poses and exaggerated, readable expressions
- Simple painted-background feel behind cel-flat characters
- Rounded panel energy; bouncy speech bubbles; sound effects permitted when the action demands them

## When to Use

- All-ages adventure, comedy, and high-energy action
- When the reference image's cues suggest playfulness, motion, or bright optimism
- Pairs natively with `gag-escalation` and `setup-reinforce-turnaround`

## Integration

- Requires `comic-character-consistency-system` — cel styles live or die on model-sheet discipline (animation's original consistency system)
- Uses `comic-style-memory-system` for outline weight and palette rules
