# Autobio Indie Literary Comic

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Personal, hand-drawn line quality
- Memoir register and introspective tone
- Caption-heavy narrative approach
- Raw, honest emotional expression
- Minimalist but expressive backgrounds

## When to Use
- Personal stories, memoirs, or literary fiction
- When the reference image supports intimate, reflective moments

## Integration
- Requires `comic-core` emotional arc rules
- Works with `comic-long-sequence-orchestrator` for long personal arcs

---

*Autobio treats the self as the story.*
