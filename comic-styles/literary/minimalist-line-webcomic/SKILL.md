# Minimalist Line Webcomic

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Thin, uniform pen lines; figures reduced to essential gesture (stick or near-stick)
- No shading, no texture, no backgrounds beyond a single essential prop or line
- Vast deliberate white space; composition is placement, not rendering
- Black on white only; panel borders thin or absent
- Deadpan, precise lettering; the gag or insight lives in timing and word choice

## When to Use

- Concept-driven humor, observational or philosophical strips
- When the reference image's cues suggest wit or quiet absurdity over atmosphere
- Pairs natively with `gag-escalation` and `silent-strip` patterns

## Integration

- Requires `comic-core` and the `comic-direction` layer — with no rendering to hide behind, the shot plan and dialogue beat ARE the comic
- Minimal load on `comic-consistency`: identity is silhouette + one signature mark
