# Bold Woodcut Adventure

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Relief print aesthetic with ink squeeze
- Strong, graphic silhouettes
- Wood grain directionality in shading
- Folkloric, mythic atmosphere
- Limited but bold color palette (if color)

## When to Use
- Mythic, folk, or adventure stories
- When the reference image supports a hand-crafted, printmaking feel

## Integration
- Requires `comic-style-memory-system` for texture and grain rules
- Works well with `comic-long-sequence-orchestrator`

---

*Woodcut treats every mark as carved.*
