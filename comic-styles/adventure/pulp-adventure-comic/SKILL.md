# Pulp Adventure Comic

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Warm, saturated color palette
- Bold, dynamic staging
- Dramatic lighting and action poses
- Classic pulp illustration energy
- Clear hero/villain visual language

## When to Use
- Action-adventure, swashbuckling, or heroic stories
- When the reference image suggests energy and movement

## Integration
- Requires `comic-style-memory-system` for color and staging rules
- Works with `comic-long-sequence-orchestrator` for serial adventures

---

*Pulp is about momentum and spectacle.*
