# Gekiga Cinematic Manga

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Realistic character proportions and anatomy
- Heavy ink brush, crosshatch, and spot blacks for shading
- Cinematic panel layouts (closeups, wide shots, depth staging)
- Deliberate, film-like pacing and composition
- Detailed urban/gritty environmental realism

## When to Use
- When the story requires a darker, more mature, or noir tone
- When cinematic framing and depth staging are important
- For stories with serious or gritty subject matter

## Key Technical Rules (from research)
- Heavy spot-black rendering via luminance thresholding
- Crosshatching density increases with emotional intensity
- Wide-angle lens distortion in appropriate shots
- Panel bleeding for key action moments

## Integration
- Requires `comic-consistency` (especially style memory for hatching behavior)
- Uses World Bible for environmental and lighting rules

---

*Gekiga treats the panel as a film frame, not a cartoon box.*
