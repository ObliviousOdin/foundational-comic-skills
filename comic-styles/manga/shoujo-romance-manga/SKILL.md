# Shoujo Romance Manga

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Classic shōjo manga aesthetic (1970s–90s influenced)
- Black and white with optional selective screentone for flowers and sparkles
- Large, multi-highlight sparkling eyes; delicate, varied line weight
- Floral and bubble decorative backgrounds expressing emotion
- Soft, layered hair rendering; elegant costume silhouettes

## When to Use
- Emotional, romantic, or introspective stories
- When the reference image suggests shyness, longing, or gentle determination

## Integration
- Requires `comic-consistency` for emotional expression handling
- Uses World Bible for mood and setting cues
