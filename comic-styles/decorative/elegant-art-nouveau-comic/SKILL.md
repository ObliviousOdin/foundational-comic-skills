# Elegant Art Nouveau Comic

**Style Lock (do not deviate)**

- Flowing, organic curves and whiplash lines
- Ornamental borders and decorative elements
- Elegant, stylized figures
- Rich but harmonious color palettes
- Emphasis on beauty and natural forms

## When to Use
- Romantic, fantastical, or decorative stories
- When the reference image supports elegance and ornamentation

## Integration
- Requires `comic-style-memory-system` for curve and ornament rules
- Works well with emotional and romantic styles

---

*Art Nouveau treats the panel as a decorative object.*
